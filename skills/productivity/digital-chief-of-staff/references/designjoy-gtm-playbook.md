# DesignJoy-Inspired Zero-Capital GTM Playbook

For B2B professional services launching with $0 marketing budget. Adapted from Brett Williams' DesignJoy model.

## Core Mechanics

| Principle | How It Translates |
|-----------|-------------------|
| Single landing page, one offer | One page, one price, one CTA. No tiers, no custom quoting. |
| No-brainer guarantee | "Try it free for 48 hours. Cancel anytime. No credit card." Risk entirely on seller. |
| Waitlist gates everything | 3 pilots → 10 founding clients → raise price → scale. Demand manages supply. |
| No meetings ever | All communication async (email, Loom, portal). Meetings are a dealbreaker filter. |
| Price on the page | Transparent pricing. No "contact us" or demo request. |
| Building in public | CEO builds on X/Twitter daily: process, data, lessons. No pitch slop. |
| $0 ad spend | Organic only: X, Reddit, LinkedIn, trade pub comments. |

## The Funnel

```
Landing Page → "Get started for free" 
  → Waitlist (scored by ICP fit)
    → Pilot (3, free/discounted, feedback-intensive)
      → Founding Clients (10, low price, case studies)
        → Scale (raise price, add tiers, grow 20+)
```

## Pipeline Channels ($0 Cost)

| Channel | Tactic | Volume Target |
|---------|--------|---------------|
| **X/Twitter** | CEO builds in public daily — dashboards, timelapses, lessons | 500-2K followers in 2 months |
| **Reddit** | Engage niche subreddits by answering questions, DM pain-point matches | 3-5 DMs/day |
| **LinkedIn** | Brand page + CEO posts on industry pain points | Comments → waitlist |
| **Trade pubs** | Insightful comments on industry articles | 1-2 clicks/day |

## The Guarantee

> "Try it free. If you don't see value in 48 hours, you owe nothing. No meetings. No credit card. We mean it."

Cost to deliver the free trial: ~$0.40 in API calls. LTV of a converting client: $14,400+/yr.

## Phased Pricing

| Phase | Clients | Price | Goal |
|-------|---------|-------|------|
| Pilots | 3 | Free/$500 | Validate product, collect case studies |
| Founding | 10 | Below target | Prove repeatable delivery, build pipeline |
| Scale | 20+ | Target + tiers | Add premium tiers, grow MRR |

## When Revenue Supports Paid Acquisition
