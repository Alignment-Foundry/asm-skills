# Agentic GTM Playbook: Zero-Capital Customer Acquisition

## When to Use This

Your Paperclip company needs to acquire its first customers but has:
- No marketing budget ($0 ad spend)
- No founder sales network (warm contacts don't exist)
- A productized service priced $1,000-1,500/mo (low-touch, needs volume)
- Agents who can execute outreach but cannot attend meetings or make calls
- A founder who chairs the board and does not operate day-to-day

This playbook is the DesignJoy model (Brett Williams) adapted for AI-run companies.

---

## The Core Pattern

```
Landing page (single offer, price on page, no-brainer guarantee)
  → Waitlist (gated by ICP fit)
    → 3 pilots (free/discounted, feedback-intensive)
      → 10 founding clients ($1,200-1,500/mo)
        → Improve product → Raise price → Add tiers → Scale 20+
```

**Key constraint:** Every step is agent-executed. The human board only provisions accounts (domains, email infrastructure) and handles legal/compliance.

## Phase A: Pipeline Channels ($0 Cost)

### Channel 1: Building in Public on X/Twitter

**Who does it:** The Paperclip CEO agent (give them a name — e.g. "Morgan Lee")

**What they do:**
- Daily posts sharing: anonymized dashboards (blurred data), processing timelapses, business-building lessons, metrics from the waitlist, client wins (anonymized)
- Follows the Brett Williams playbook: transparent, opinionated, shows the work
- Links in bio → landing page

**Target:** 500-2,000 followers in 2 months. Direct DMs from ICP-fit prospects.

**Tone:** Builder mode. "Here's what I learned processing 3 business P&Ls today." Not salesy.

### Channel 2: Niche Community Engagement (Reddit)

**Who does it:** The Communication Agent (give them a name — e.g. "Taylor Kim")

**What they do:**
- Monitor the niche-specific subreddit (e.g. r/smallbusiness, r/freelance, r/agency)
- Answer financial/operational questions with genuine value
- DM anyone describing the exact pain the product solves
- Never hard-sell — lead with curiosity: "Saw your comment about X. We built a dashboard for that. Want a free look?"

**Target:** 3-5 DMs/day. Each DM is a real reply to a real comment they already made.

### Channel 3: LinkedIn Brand Presence

**Who does it:** The CEO agent + Communication Agent

**What they do:**
- 2 posts/week from the company brand page
- Topics: niche-specific financial/operational insights
- Use verbatim language from AI discovery to make posts land
- Engage in comments on relevant posts from industry influencers

**Target:** Inbound comments → profile visits → waitlist signups.

### Channel 4: Trade Publication Comments

**Who does it:** Communication Agent

**What they do:**
- Monitor trade publications in the niche
- Leave insightful comments on relevant articles
- Include a natural link to the 48-hour free trial landing page
- One insightful comment per week per publication

**Target:** 1-2 clicks/day from curated comment readers.

## Phase B: The Offer Design

### The Landing Page (Single Page, One Offer)

Follow the DesignJoy format:
- **Headline:** What you do, who it's for
- **Bullet list of deliverables:** Weekly dashboard, monthly narrative, quarterly review, email support
- **Price:** On the page. No custom quoting.
- **Call to action:** "Get your first dashboard in 48 hours for free"
- **Risk reversal:** "Upload one month of statements. We deliver your dashboard. If it doesn't change how you see your business, you owe nothing. No questions. No meetings."
- **Scarcity:** "Only 10 founding client spots available at this price."
- **Social proof:** "X businesses are already using this." (from pilots)

### The "No-Brainer" Guarantee

> "Upload one month of business statements. We process them and deliver your first financial dashboard within 48 hours. If it doesn't change how you see your business, you owe nothing. No questions. No meetings. We mean it."

**Why this works:**
- The cost to the company is negligible (~$0.40 in API calls per dashboard)
- The lifetime value of a converting client is $14,400+/yr
- The offer is so low-risk that "no" is irrational — and once they see the dashboard, they're hooked

### The Waitlist

- Captured from the landing page: email + business name + revenue range + biggest pain point (dropdown)
- CEO agent reviews daily — prioritizes by ICP fit
- Communication agent sends personal email: "Thanks for joining. You're #X. Here's what to expect."
- When a spot opens: "Your founding spot is available. 48 hours to claim it."

## Phase C: Pricing & Rollout

| Phase | Timeline | Clients | Price | Goal |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Pilots** | Weeks 1-4 | 3 | Free/$500 | Validate product, collect case studies |
| **Founding** | Weeks 5-12 | 10 | $1,200/mo | Prove repeatable delivery, build pipeline |
| **Scale** | Weeks 13-24 | 20+ | $1,800+ (Tier 1) | Add Tier 2 ($2,500), Tier 3 ($3,500) |

**Price anchoring:** DesignJoy started at $449/mo and raised to $5,495/mo as demand justified it. Start below your target price to fill the waitlist faster and build case studies. Raise with each cohort.

## Phase D: Sending Infrastructure (for When Pipeline Justifies It)

When revenue supports scaling beyond organic channels, warm these sending domains before launching cold outreach:

| Domain | Purpose | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **Primary** (e.g. branddash.com) | Main sender for email sequences | Register at cheap registrar ($10/yr) |
| **Secondary** (e.g. productforniche.com) | Backup sender | Descriptive, keyword-rich |
| **Backup** (e.g. brandintel.com) | Third sender | Short, professional |

**Strategy:**
- 3 domains = 3 independent sending reputations
- If one gets flagged, the other two keep running
- Main brand domain (e.g. brand.com) stays reputation-clean for public landing page
- Warm each domain for 2+ weeks on Instantly.ai before any campaign
- Start at 10 emails/day, ramp by 5/day weekly

**Cost when ready:** ~$100-130/mo total (Apollo.io $49/mo + Instantly.ai $47/mo)

## Agent Role Assignments (GTM Mode)

| Agent | GTM Responsibility | Channels |
|---|---|---|
| **CEO Agent** | Pipeline oversight, built-in-public content, X/Twitter presence, waitlist review | X/Twitter, LinkedIn (brand page) |
| **Communication Agent** | Community engagement, DM outreach, reply handling, trade pub comments | Reddit, LinkedIn DMs, trade pubs |
| **Financial Intelligence Agent** | Sample dashboard generation for landing page, pilot delivery | Product (not outreach) |
| **Document Processing Agent** | Pilot statement processing — the 48-hour turnaround promise | Product (not outreach) |
| **Operations Agent** | Waitlist tracking, conversion metrics, weekly pipeline report | Internal dashboards |
| **Onboarding Agent** | Pilot intake sequence, founding client onboarding | Welcome emails, DocuSign |

## Key Metrics to Track

| Metric | Target | Tracked By |
|---|---|---|
| Waitlist signups/week | 5-10 | Operations Agent |
| DMs sent/day | 3-5 | Communication Agent |
| X/Twitter followers | 500+ by week 8 | CEO Agent |
| Pilot-to-founder conversion | 2 of 3 | CEO Agent |
| Founder churn (first 3 months) | <20% | Operations Agent |
| Referral rate (from founders) | >0.5 referrals/client | Communication Agent |

## Pitfalls

- **Don't start cold outreach before organic channels are working.** Cold email at $1,500/mo with zero brand trust converts at <0.5%. Community + built-in-public creates the trust that makes outbound work later.
- **Don't use the main brand domain for cold sending.** If {business-domain-1} gets flagged as spam, the entire public-facing brand is burned. Use separate sending domains.
- **Don't skip the pilot phase.** Three free pilots catch product gaps before you charge real money. The feedback from 3 real users is worth more than 100 assumptions.
- **Don't open founding client spots before pilots validate the product.** If the 48-hour turnaround promise fails on pilot 1, fix it before you let anyone pay.
- **Don't make the founder the CEO or salesperson.** The human is Board. The CEO is an agent. If the founder starts doing sales calls, the async model is broken — you're now a consultancy with an AI wrapper.
- **Don't make multiple offers on one landing page.** One price, one CTA, one risk reversal. Multiple tier options create analysis paralysis. Add tiers only after founding cohort validates demand.
- **Don't let agents post as the human founder's personal account.** Use company brand accounts. The human's personal identity shouldn't be the product.
