## State Market Share Data

### Texas (TX DOI 2024)
- **Personal Auto**: Progressive 20.97%, State Farm 16.85%, Allstate 7.86%, GEICO 8.19%
- **Homeowners**: State Farm Lloyds 19.47%, Allstate 12.22%, Travelers 5.05%, USAA 3.80%, Texas Farmers 3.60%
- **Workers Comp**: Texas Mutual 39.40% (dominant), Zurich 3.90%, Arch 1.92%
- **Total P&C market**: $35.4B auto, $18.95B homeowners
- **Premiums**: Auto avg ~$2,200/yr, HO avg ~$3,347/yr (State Farm cheapest at $3,347 for $300k dwelling)

### Georgia (US News 2026 rate data)
- **Auto**: State Farm 21% market share (largest)
- **Homeowners**: State Farm $758/yr (cheapest for $300k dwelling), USAA $1,572, Auto-Owners $1,792, Allstate $1,618, Travelers $2,592
- **Average HO premium**: $2,136/yr (range $758-$2,592)
- **Risk factors**: Hurricane season June-Nov, tornadoes, floods, wildfires
- **FAIR Plan**: Georgia Underwriting Association (GUA)

### Ohio/Indiana Region (OH DOI 2024)
- **Top 10 P&C groups in OH**: State Farm 12.5% ($3.13B), Progressive 8.7% ($2.17B), Allstate 6.4% ($1.61B), Liberty Mutual 4.9%, Cincinnati Financial 4.3% ($1.09B), Erie 4.0% ($1.01B), Berkshire Hathaway 3.8%, Nationwide 3.3%, Travelers 3.3%, Grange 2.9%
- **Midwest regional carriers**: Auto-Owners ($619M, 2.5%), Westfield ($639M, 2.5%), Ohio Mutual, Encova, Acuity, Celina, Frankenmuth
- **Auto avg**: ~$1,500/yr (OH less than TX)
- **HO avg**: ~$2,200/yr (IN median, less than TX/GA coastal risk)
